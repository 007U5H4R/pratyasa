## Design Specificity Verdict
"Identity is authored. Composition is rented." Product-specific: the embossed wordmark, gold/black from the physical case, the hero cutout with a real LCD reading, the four-stage mechanism SVG, cyan restricted to the device's own display, the Sanskrit gloss. Category-default: the entire skeleton — sticky blurred header, 1140px centred column, eyebrow/h1/lede/two-pill CTA, 3-up stat bar, 01–07 kickers, bento, 2-card evidence row, fade-up reveals. Only the SVG resisted a swap into an unrelated product.

## Priority Issues
- **[P1] Diagram dimmed itself into illegibility.** Inactive stages at .46 opacity put sub-labels at 1.84:1. The one original element on the page read as a broken graphic. Fixed: resting opacity .8, active marked by gold fill and a tinted hit area rather than by dimming its neighbours.
- **[P1] `--text-3` failed WCAG AA.** 4.43:1 on --bg, 4.14:1 on --surface at 13px, covering every bento label, caption and the regulatory disclaimer. Fixed: #918c83, measured 5.89:1 and 5.50:1.
- **[P1] Conversion was a naked mailto.** Address existed only inside the href. Fixed: a contact card printing the address as selectable text, plus the Indian Patent Office register link for verification.
- **[P1] Eight undersized tap targets at 375px.** Nav links 36px tall, brand logo 132x13, text links 18px tall. Fixed: all now clear 44x44; re-measured 0 undersized at 375/768/1280.
- **[P2] Bento void.** `.cell-media` spanned 2 rows where the grid needed 3, leaving a ~360x340 hole right after "Measured, published, peer-reviewed". Fixed: 3x2 with the photo spanning both rows, and the samples cell spanning 2 columns at the 2-column breakpoint.
- **[P2] Fold wasted half the screen.** Device and stat bar both sat below the fold. Fixed by compressing hero vertical rhythm, not by shrinking the device into a column.
- **[P2] Tabbing the steps strobed the diagram.** A focus listener re-fired setActive on every Tab. Fixed: click only; buttons already emit click on Enter/Space.
- **[P2] Nav current state was paint-only.** `.is-current` set no `aria-current`, inconsistent with the step pattern in the same file. Fixed.
